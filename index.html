Privacy Policy for Learn OS

Effective Date: May 7, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Learn OS ("we," "our," or "us"). This privacy policy explains how our educational mobile application handles your information when you use our app.

Learn OS is an educational app designed to help students learn Operating System concepts. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

No personal information is collected - We do not require registration, email, or any personal details to use our app.

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: We may collect device model, OS version, and app version for analytics
  • Usage Data: Lesson progress, quiz scores, and coin balance (stored locally on your device only)
  • Advertising ID: Google AdMob may collect advertising ID to serve relevant ads

2.3 Data Storage

  • All your progress (lessons completed, quiz scores, coins) is stored locally on your device using SharedPreferences
  • We do not store any user data on our servers

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Track your lesson progress within the app
  • Award coins for completing lessons and quizzes
  • Display relevant advertisements
  • Improve app performance and user experience

4. Advertising

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements in our app.

Ad Types Used:

  • Banner Ads: Displayed at the bottom of screens
  • Interstitial Ads: Full-screen ads shown occasionally
  • Rewarded Ads: Optional ads that users can watch to earn bonus coins

Google AdMob may collect:

  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • Advertising ID
  • IP address
  • App usage data

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Opt-out of Personalized Ads:

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization
